Calibration

LR/UD move the eyes (used by the Look joystick above). TL/BL are the left eye's upper/lower eyelids; TR/BR are the right eye's — these are what Blink, Wink, Sleep, Wake, and Expression actually move. If one eye's eyelids move the wrong direction (e.g. closing on one side while opening on the other during Blink), toggle Inverted on that eye's TL/BL or TR/BR channels, not LR/UD.

Save persists immediately and applies right away — there's no preview-without-saving. Workflow: adjust a value, Save, then use the Look joystick or Actions/Expression buttons above to see the effect, and repeat.
